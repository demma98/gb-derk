--- features ---
- 18 different mazes
- sound effects
- animated title screen
- ending screen with "total moves" counter
- can read mazes in the format used by CX16-MAZE

--- shortcuts ---
- can restart by pressing START+SELECT+B+A

--- compile ---
to compile you'll need rgbasm installed, then it's as simple as
>make
